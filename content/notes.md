This harbour was drawn for WhatIIIF, to show what notes on a picture, and a tour through them, can do. Take the
tour, or choose a note: each takes the viewer to its part of the picture.

## [The lighthouse](https://gjhilton.github.io/whatiiif-demo-notes/?viewer=openseadragon#xywh=750,750,1560,3045)

Red and white, on a rock at the harbour mouth, its lamp lit for the evening. A note can be about anything you would
point at if you were standing beside someone.

## [Gulls](https://gjhilton.github.io/whatiiif-demo-notes/?viewer=openseadragon#xywh=2700,960,2910,1020)

Five of them, heading home.

## [The fishing fleet](https://gjhilton.github.io/whatiiif-demo-notes/?viewer=openseadragon#xywh=3060,3510,3540,1200)

Three boats under red, teal and orange sails. A note can be as long as you like, with *emphasis*,
[links](https://iiif.io) and lists, written as in the rest of Markdown.

## [The pier](https://gjhilton.github.io/whatiiif-demo-notes/?viewer=openseadragon#xywh=7050,3540,1950,450)

Where the boats tie up. Anyone can share a view like this one with **Link to this view**, which is also how a note
says where it points.

## [A message in a bottle](https://gjhilton.github.io/whatiiif-demo-notes/?viewer=openseadragon#xywh=2325,5010,330,180)

Too small to read until you zoom all the way in: the picture is 9,000 pixels across, and every one of them is there.
